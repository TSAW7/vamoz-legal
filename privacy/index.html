Who we are

VAMOZ: Side Quests ("VAMOZ", "we") is an iOS app, currently in beta, that gives you real-world side quests to go do. It's built and run by Theo Sawyers, a developer based in Massachusetts, USA.

This policy explains what we collect while you test VAMOZ, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, and we never will.

How we use it

  • Run the app: your account, feed, profile, leaderboard, and notifications.
  • Pick your quests: your interests, location, side quest answer, and quest history decide what shows up when you spin.
  • Check proof and keep things safe: proof posts and comments are checked automatically to confirm a quest was done and to filter out inappropriate content. Unclear cases may be reviewed by hand.
  • Improve VAMOZ: usage data and feedback show us what's working, what's broken, and which quests to write next.
  • Email you about the app: occasional beta updates, like new features or new quests for your city. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we'll honor it right away. We won't send ads for other companies.

Featuring public posts

VAMOZ posts content on its own social media. If you make a post public, you agree that we may feature it (for example, in a "coolest quest of the week" video), credited to your username. We'll try to message you first. Friends-only and Classified posts are never featured. If you want a featured post taken down, email us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Post visibility: choose public, friends-only, or Classified (only you) for every post, and change it any time.
  • Location: turn location access off in your phone's Settings. You can still use the app, with fewer nearby quests.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in Settings.
  • Emails: we'll only email you updates if you tick the box at sign-up, and you can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Delete your data: tap Delete account in Settings, or email vamozapp@gmail.com. We delete your account and everything tied to it within 30 days.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except for anything we must keep by law. Usage data may be kept in a form that doesn't identify you.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use VAMOZ. If we learn someone under 13 has signed up, we'll delete their account.

Accounts for people aged 13 to 17 get extra protections. Their posts start as Classified, only friends can message them, their location is never shown to other users, and adult-only quests are hidden from them.

Security

Your data is stored with Supabase, protected by access rules and encrypted connections. No app is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes to this policy

This is a beta policy and it will change before public launch. If we change it in a big way, we'll tell you in the app or by email.
